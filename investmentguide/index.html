1. Executive Summary

Why Vietnam. Why Now. Why Hi-Tech.

Vietnam is moving from low-cost assembly toward high-value technology manufacturing. Hi-tech, semiconductors, AI and advanced manufacturing are national priorities through 2030 and beyond, and the Investment Law 2025 provides the most investor-friendly incentive framework in the country's history.

  1. Up to 30 years at 10% CIT for qualifying hi-tech projects.
  2. A Special Investment Procedure that removes EIA, construction permits and several approval layers.
  3. Location selection is the most consequential early decision.

2. The Hi-Tech Opportunity

Supply chains are restructuring under US–China tensions and China+1 strategies: Taiwanese semiconductor supply chains are diversifying into Vietnam, Japanese and Korean groups are investing in automation and EV components, and demand for AI data centers is growing – with Vietnam as a frontrunner.

Vietnam today: Samsung (smartphone complexes in Bac Ninh and Thai Nguyen), Intel (its largest Southeast Asian assembly & test facility, in Ho Chi Minh City), Foxconn, Pegatron, Quanta, Amkor, LG, Bosch and Panasonic – an existing supplier base, a proven electronics and semiconductor ecosystem and a clear government commitment to upgrading.

3. Government Strategy & Policy Direction

  • Hi-tech manufacturing, semiconductors, AI and data centers are national strategic priority sectors.
  • Target: hi-tech exports above 50% of total export value by 2030.
  • An approved national semiconductor strategy aligns talent, infrastructure and incentives.
  • Special Economic Zones and Hi-Tech Parks receive priority infrastructure investment.
  • Regulatory posture: pro-FDI and competitive with Thailand, Malaysia and Indonesia.

4. Investment Incentives (Investment Law 2025)

IncentiveStandard (priority sectors, industrial zones)Special (semiconductor, AI, large R&D, data centers, ≥ VND 6,000 bn)
CIT rate10% for 15 years10% for up to 30 years
Exemption4 years full exemption + 50% reduction for 9 yearsSame schedule
R&D–R&D expenditure deductible at 200%
  • Import duty: permanent exemption for fixed-asset machinery and equipment; 5-year exemption for raw materials and components for hi-tech production.
  • Land: reduced or exempted land rent depending on sector and location; hi-tech SMEs and startups in industrial parks get at least 30% reduction for the first 5 years.
  • Personal income tax: experts and scientists at R&D centers, hi-tech parks and startups – 2-year exemption then 50% reduction for 4 years; individual investors in tech startups – 100% exemption on capital gains from share transfers.

6. Talent Pool

  • Education: ranked 31st of 81 systems in OECD PISA 2022; literacy around 96%; strong STEM emphasis.
  • Output: 400,000–500,000 STEM graduates and 50,000+ IT graduates per year; a national program to train 50,000 semiconductor engineers by 2030.
  • Workforce: about 65 million working-age people, median age about 33, roughly 1 million new workers per year.

7. Infrastructure for Hi-Tech Operations

  • Power: national grid coverage across industrial zones, dedicated substations in Grade-A parks, DPPA for direct renewable purchases.
  • Connectivity: fiber in major parks, 5G in key corridors, submarine cable landings in Da Nang and Vung Tau.
  • Specialized facilities: clean rooms in select hi-tech parks (Saigon Hi-Tech Park, Hoa Lac), built-to-suit clean-room options, ultra-pure water on request.
  • Logistics: air freight via Noi Bai, Tan Son Nhat and Long Thanh; same-day trucking from northern parks to Noi Bai; about 14 days from Cai Mep–Thi Vai to the US West Coast.

8. Legal & Regulatory Framework

  • Structures: 100% foreign-owned company (LLC or JSC), joint venture, R&D center, or representative office (market research only).
  • Setup sequence: Investment Registration Certificate (IRC) → Enterprise Registration Certificate (ERC) → sector licenses where required. Typically 2–4 months inside an industrial park.
  • Investment Policy Approval (IPA): only for large or sensitive projects – not most industrial-park investments.
  • M&A: registration required above 50% ownership, for restricted sectors and for border or island land.
  • IP: member of TRIPS, PCT and the Berne Convention; enforcement is improving.

9. Special Investment Procedure

Eligible locations: industrial parks, export processing zones, hi-tech parks, free trade zones and international financial centers.

Waived approvals: IPA, technology assessment, EIA, detailed planning approval, construction permit and fire-prevention (PCCC) permit. Investors provide a compliance commitment and a commencement notification – IEP handles eligibility, documentation and submission.

Practical effect: shorter pre-construction timelines, fewer government touchpoints and lower setup risk.

11. ESG & Sustainability

  • Net Zero 2050 commitment (COP26) and PDP8, targeting 47% renewable energy by 2030.
  • DPPA for eligible manufacturers; LEED, EDGE and ISO 14001 increasingly available in premium parks.
  • EU CBAM: exporters to the EU should start carbon-footprint assessment now.
  • ESG disclosure is not yet mandatory for foreign firms in Vietnam, but parent-company obligations (EU CSRD, IFRS S1/S2, SEC) increasingly cover Vietnamese operations.
